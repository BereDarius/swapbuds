# SwapBuds - Legal & Compliance Guide (Romania)

Complete legal framework for launching and operating a peer-to-peer trading platform in Romania.

---

## Legal Structure

### Business Entity Options

**Option 1: SRL (Societate cu Răspundere Limitată) - RECOMMENDED**

**Pros:**

- Personal liability protection (limited to share capital)
- Flexible management structure
- Professional credibility with users and partners
- Standard business form in Romania
- Can have 1-50 shareholders

**Cons:**

- Minimum share capital: 200 RON (required)
- Annual financial statements required
- More complex accounting than PFA
- Annual costs: ~1,500-3,000 RON/yr (accounting)

**Best for:** Small to medium platforms, bootstrapping, single founder or small team

**Setup:**

- Cost: ~500-1,500 RON (notary, registration, legal fees)
- Minimum capital: 200 RON
- Time: 3-10 days (with electronic registration)
- Registration at: ONRC (Oficiul Național al Registrului Comerțului)

**Requirements:**

- Romanian fiscal address (can be virtual office)
- At least one administrator
- Articles of Association (Act Constitutiv)
- Unique company name check

---

**Option 2: SA (Societate pe Acțiuni)**

**Pros:**

- Best for raising venture capital
- Stock options for employees
- Clear ownership structure (shares)
- Unlimited growth potential
- Can be listed on stock exchange

**Cons:**

- High minimum capital: 90,000 RON
- More expensive to maintain (5,000-10,000 RON/yr)
- Mandatory board of directors (3+ members)
- Mandatory auditor
- Complex compliance requirements

**Best for:** Large-scale startups seeking significant VC funding

**Only choose if:**

- Planning to raise >500,000 EUR in funding
- Want to offer stock options to team
- Targeting IPO on BVB (Bucharest Stock Exchange)
- Need more than 50 shareholders

---

**Option 3: PFA (Persoană Fizică Autorizată)**

**Pros:**

- Simplest to start
- Lower registration costs (~300-600 RON)
- Simplified accounting (for income <300,000 RON/yr)
- Complete control
- Can choose norm de venit (standard deduction) if eligible

**Cons:**

- **No liability protection** (personal assets at risk)
- Harder to raise money from investors
- Less professional credibility
- Limited to one person (can't have partners)
- Less suitable for tech platforms

**NOT RECOMMENDED for SwapBuds** - Too much liability risk with user-generated content and peer-to-peer transactions. SRL provides much better protection.

---

### Recommended Approach

**Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Start as SRL**

- [ ] Check company name availability at ONRC
- [ ] Prepare Articles of Association (Act Constitutiv)
- [ ] Deposit 200 RON minimum capital in bank
- [ ] Register at ONRC (online or through notary)
- [ ] Get CUI (Cod Unic de Înregistrare) - Tax ID
- [ ] Register at ANAF (Romanian Tax Authority)
- [ ] Open business bank account
- [ ] Register with Romanian Trade Register
- Cost: 500-1,500 RON one-time, ~1,500-3,000 RON/yr (accounting)

**Phase 2 (If raising significant funding): Convert to SA**

- [ ] Only if seeking >500,000 EUR in VC funding
- [ ] Hire lawyer for conversion (~15,000-25,000 RON)
- [ ] Increase capital to 90,000 RON minimum
- [ ] Appoint board of directors (3+ members)
- [ ] Issue shares and set up cap table

## Required Legal Documents

### 1. Terms of Service (TOS)

**Must Include:**

- Eligibility (age 18+, or 13+ with parent consent)
- Account registration and security
- User conduct rules
- Prohibited items (weapons, illegal items, hazardous)
- Content ownership and licensing
- SwapBuds' rights to moderate/remove content
- Limitation of liability
- Dispute resolution process
- Termination rights
- Changes to terms

**Key Clauses:**

**User Responsibility:**

```
"SwapBuds is a platform that connects users for peer-to-peer
trading. We do not take possession of items, verify item
condition, or participate in transactions. Users are solely
responsible for their trades."
```

**No Warranty:**

```
"SwapBuds is provided 'as is' without warranties. We do not
guarantee the accuracy of listings, identity of users, or
successful completion of trades."
```

**Limitation of Liability:**

```
"SwapBuds is not liable for disputes between users, lost or
damaged items, fraudulent users, or failed transactions.
Maximum liability is limited to the fees paid (if any) in
the past 12 months."
```

**Resources:**

- TermsFeed ($200-500 for custom TOS)
- Termly (free generator, $200/yr for custom)
- Lawyer review ($500-1,500 recommended)

### 2. Privacy Policy

**Must Comply With:**

- **GDPR** (EU Regulation 2016/679) - Mandatory for all EU/Romanian companies
- **Romanian Law 190/2018** - GDPR implementation in Romania
- **ANSPDCP** (Romanian Data Protection Authority) regulations
- **ePrivacy Directive** - Cookie consent and electronic communications

**Must Include:**

- What data you collect (email, location, usage data)
- How you use data (account management, matching, analytics)
- Third-party services (auth, analytics, email)
- Cookie usage and tracking
- User rights (access, deletion, correction)
- Data retention periods
- International transfers (if applicable)
- Contact for privacy questions

**Key Sections:**

**Data Collection:**

```
We collect:
- Account information (email, username, location)
- Profile data (bio, interests, trade history)
- Item listings (photos, descriptions)
- Messages between users
- Usage data (clicks, searches, time on site)
- Device information (IP address, browser, OS)
```

**Data Usage:**

```
We use your data to:
- Provide and improve the service
- Match you with relevant trade opportunities
- Send notifications about your trades
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Analyze platform performance
- Comply with legal obligations
```

**User Rights (GDPR):**

```
You have the right to:
- Access your data (Art. 15 GDPR)
- Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
- Erasure / "Right to be forgotten" (Art. 17)
- Data portability (Art. 20)
- Object to processing (Art. 21)
- Restrict processing (Art. 18)
- Lodge complaint with ANSPDCP (Romanian DPA)
```

**Resources:**

- iubenda (€27-200/yr) - GDPR compliant, Romanian language support
- GDPR.eu Privacy Policy Generator (free)
- Romanian lawyer review (1,000-3,000 RON recommended)

### 5. Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy

**Required:** If users can upload content

**Must Comply With:**

- **Romanian Copyright Law (Legea 8/1996)**
- **EU Copyright Directive (2019/790)**
- **E-Commerce Directive (2000/31/EC)**

**Must Include:**

- Designated contact for copyright complaints
- How to file copyright infringement notice
- Counter-notification process
- Repeat infringer policy
- Notice-and-takedown procedure

**No Registration Required:**

- Unlike DMCA, no agent registration needed
- Simply provide contact email in policy
- Respond to takedown requests within 24-48 hours
- Document all complaints and actions taken

**Key Contact:**

- ORDA (Romanian Copyright Office) - for major disputes
- Email: office@orda.ro

## Compliance Requirements

### Data Protection (GDPR - Romanian Implementation)

**GDPR Compliance (MANDATORY in Romania):**

**Requirements:**

- [ ] Legal basis for processing data (consent, contract, legitimate interest)
- [ ] Privacy policy in Romanian language (mandatory)
- [ ] Data processing agreements with vendors (Stripe, AWS, etc.)
- [ ] Right to access, rectification, deletion, portability (Art. 15-20)
- [ ] Right to object and restrict processing (Art. 21-22)
- [ ] Data breach notification to ANSPDCP within 72 hours
- [ ] Notify affected users if high risk
- [ ] Appoint DPO if: processing sensitive data at scale, public authority, or large-scale monitoring
- [ ] Register with ANSPDCP (Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal)
- [ ] Maintain processing register (Art. 30)

**ANSPDCP Contact:**

- Website: dataprotection.ro
- Email: anspdcp@dataprotection.ro
- Address: B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru 28-30, Sector 1, București

**Penalties:**

- GDPR: Up to €20M or 4% of annual revenue (whichever higher)
- ANSPDCP can issue fines, warnings, or processing bans
- **Mitigation:** Implement data rights from day one, maintain documentation

**Tools:**

- Mine (data rights automation, $99/mo)
- Osano (consent + privacy, $500/mo)
- DIY: Build data export and deletion features

---

### Age Verification

**Romanian Law & GDPR (Children's Data Protection):**

**Applies if:** Users under 16 allowed (GDPR Art. 8)

**Requirements:**

- Romania follows GDPR: minimum age 16 for consent
- Under 16: verifiable parental consent required
- Don't collect more data than necessary from minors
- No behavioral advertising to children
- Strict data security and processing limitations

**Recommendation for SwapBuds:**

- **Set minimum age to 18+** (easiest and safest for trading platform)
- Or allow 16+ without parental consent (GDPR compliant)
- Include age checkbox in signup: "Am cel puțin 18 ani" (I am at least 18 years old)
- Ban users who provide false age information
- Consider ID verification for high-value trades

### Content Moderation

**E-Commerce Directive (2000/31/EC) & Digital Services Act (DSA):**

- Platforms not liable for user content if acting as intermediary
- **BUT** Must act quickly once aware of illegal content
- Must have notice-and-takedown procedures
- **DSA (2024):** New obligations for content moderation and transparency
- Romania implements via Law 365/2002 on e-commerce

**Best Practices:**

- [ ] Clear community guidelines
- [ ] User reporting system
- [ ] Automated and manual moderation
- [ ] Quick response to illegal content (<24 hours)
- [ ] Document moderation decisions
- [ ] Ban repeat offenders

**Tools:**

- Sift (fraud detection, $500+/mo)
- WebPurify (content moderation API, $99+/mo)
- DIY: User reports + manual review (start here)

### Taxes (Romanian System)

**TVA (VAT - Taxa pe Valoarea Adăugată):**

- **Standard rate: 19%** (for digital services)
- **Physical goods:** SwapBuds doesn't handle transactions, users responsible
- **Digital services (premium subscriptions):** Must charge 19% TVA

**VAT Registration:**

- Mandatory if revenue >300,000 RON/year (~60,000 EUR)
- Optional (and recommended) if lower revenue
- Register at ANAF (Romanian Tax Authority)
- Cost: Free registration

**Corporate Income Tax:**

- **Standard: 16%** on profit
- **Micro-enterprise regime:** 1-3% on revenue (if eligible)
  - Eligibility: <500,000 EUR revenue, <9 employees
  - No partners with >25% in other companies

**Solutions:**

- Use Stripe Tax (supports Romanian VAT)
- Use Romanian accounting software (e.g., SmartBill, Factura.ro)
- Hire Romanian accountant (contabil autorizat): 300-800 RON/month

**EU VAT:**

- EU reverse charge applies for B2B services
- For B2C: charge VAT based on customer's country
- Use OSS (One Stop Shop) for simplified EU VAT reporting

### Other Regulations

**Email Marketing (GDPR + ePrivacy):**

- [ ] Opt-in consent required (pre-checked boxes not allowed)
- [ ] Clear unsubscribe link in every email
- [ ] Honor opt-outs immediately
- [ ] Include company details (name, address, CUI)
- [ ] Keep records of consent
- Penalty: Up to €20M or 4% revenue for GDPR violations

**Accessibility (EU Web Accessibility Directive):**

- Public sector websites must comply (private sector encouraged)
- Follow WCAG 2.1 guidelines (AA level)
- Romania implements via Law 448/2006

- Tools: WAVE, axe DevTools (browser extensions)

**Payment Processing (PCI DSS):**

- Required if processing credit cards
- Use Stripe (PCI compliant) - you don't touch card data
- Never store card numbers yourself

## Liability Protection

### Insurance (Romanian Market)

**RCA Profesională (Professional Liability Insurance):**

- Covers: Professional errors, negligence, damages
- Cost: 1,500-4,000 RON/yr for small tech company
- Recommended when launching publicly

**Asigurare Răspundere Civilă (General Liability):**

- Covers: Third-party injury, property damage
- Cost: 1,000-3,000 RON/yr
- Consider once generating revenue

**Cyber Insurance (Asigurare Cyber):**

- Covers: Data breaches, cyber attacks, legal costs, GDPR fines
- Cost: 3,000-8,000 RON/yr
- **Highly recommended** for platforms handling user data
- Growing market in Romania

**Asigurare Manageri (D&O Insurance):**

- Only needed if raising VC or have board
- Cost: 5,000-15,000 RON/yr

**Recommended Providers (Romania):**

- Allianz-Țiriac (has cyber insurance)
- Generali România
- Groupama (professional liability)
- Asirom (wide coverage options)
- Omniasig (tech-focused products)

### Between User and SwapBuds

**Arbitration Clause (Optional but Common):**

**Pros:**

- Faster and cheaper than court
- Avoids class action lawsuits
- Neutral forum

**Cons:**

- Users may dislike feeling forced
- Can seem anti-consumer
- Some states limiting enforceability

**Example Clause:**

```
Any dispute between you and SwapBuds will be resolved through
mediation followed by arbitration under the rules of the Court
of International Commercial Arbitration (CCIR) in Bucharest,
Romania. Governing law: Romanian law.

Exception: Either party may seek injunctive relief in Romanian
courts (e.g., for IP violations).
```

**Alternative: Judecătorie (Romanian Courts)**

```
Disputes under 75,000 RON may be brought in the competent
Judecătorie (local court). Disputes over 75,000 RON go to
Tribunalul (county court).
```

**Recommendation:**

- Start without arbitration clause (more user-friendly)
- Add if facing frivolous lawsuits
- Consult lawyer before adding

## Common Legal Pitfalls to Avoid (Romania)

1. **No Terms of Service** - Users can claim anything, no protection
2. **Not having documents in Romanian** - Required by consumer law
3. **Ignoring GDPR** - Mandatory in Romania/EU, huge fines up to €20M
4. **No age verification** - GDPR violations if under-16 users without consent
5. **Personal and business mixed** - Loses SRL liability protection
6. **No insurance** - One lawsuit or data breach could bankrupt you
7. **Not registering with ANAF** - Tax evasion, severe penalties
8. **No accounting** - Mandatory for SRL, fines for non-compliance
9. **Ignoring ANSPDCP** - Data breach not reported = additional fines
10. **No lawyer review** - Romanian law has specifics templates miss

## When to Escalate Legal Issues

**Consult a Lawyer (Avocat) Immediately If:**

- Received legal threat or lawsuit (acțiune în instanță)
- Copyright takedown notice
- Data breach or hack
- ANSPDCP inquiry or investigation
- ANAF (tax authority) audit
- Investor term sheet (before signing)
- Major partnership agreement
- Significant IP dispute
- Changing business structure (SRL to SA)
- International expansion

**Cost of Ignoring:**

- GDPR violation: Up to €20M or 4% revenue
- Copyright infringement: Up to 50,000 RON + damages
- Lawsuit defense: 10,000-100,000 RON+
- Data breach: Average €3.5M (EU study)
- Tax penalties: 0.02-0.05% per day + interest
- ANAF fines: Can reach 100% of unpaid tax

**Cost of Prevention:**

- Lawyer consultation: 200-500 RON
- Legal document review: 1,000-5,000 RON
- Basic compliance: 2,000-10,000 RON/yr

**Prevention is 10-100x cheaper than fixing problems.**

## Legal Philosophy for SwapBuds

**Core Principles:**

1. **Transparency** - Be clear about what you do with data
2. **User Rights** - Make it easy to access, delete, export data
3. **Safety First** - Moderate harmful content aggressively
4. **Fair Dealing** - No hidden fees, clear terms
5. **Privacy Conscious** - Collect minimum data necessary
6. **Community First** - Guidelines that protect users

**Remember:** Good legal practices build trust, not just avoid lawsuits. Users who feel protected will become advocates for your platform.
