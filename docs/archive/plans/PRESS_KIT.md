## 🏢 Company Overview

### What is SwapBuds?

SwapBuds is a peer-to-peer trading platform designed for collectors, gamers, anime fans, and anyone looking to swap items within their community. Unlike traditional marketplaces, we focus on item-for-item exchanges rather than monetary transactions.

### Mission

To empower communities to trade items sustainably while building meaningful connections.

### Vision

To become the world's most trusted platform for peer-to-peer item trading.

### Founded

2025

### Launch Date

March 17, 2026

### Target Audience

- Collectibles enthusiasts (trading cards, figures, memorabilia)
- Gamers (video games, consoles, accessories)
- Anime and manga fans
- Vintage item collectors
- Sustainability-conscious consumers
- Gen Z and Millennials (ages 16-35)

## 📊 Market Opportunity

### Market Size

- Global collectibles market: $200B+ annually
- Online marketplace users: 2B+ worldwide
- Peer-to-peer commerce: Growing 25% YoY

### Problem We Solve

1. **Safety Concerns**: Traditional platforms lack robust safety features for P2P trading
2. **High Fees**: Listing fees and commissions eat into trade value
3. **Complex Processes**: Existing solutions are complicated and time-consuming
4. **Lack of Community**: No dedicated space for traders to connect
5. **Waste**: New consumption when items could be traded

### Our Solution

A purpose-built platform with:

- Built-in verification and dispute resolution
- Zero fees for users
- Simple 3-step trading process
- Vibrant community features
- Sustainable item circulation
