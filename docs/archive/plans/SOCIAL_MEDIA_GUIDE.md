# SwapBuds Social Media Content Guide

**Brand Voice**: Friendly, trustworthy, exciting, inclusive
**Tone**: Conversational yet professional, enthusiastic without being overwhelming

---

## 📱 Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Twitter/X

- **Character limit**: 280 (aim for 250 to allow for retweets)
- **Posting frequency**: 2-3 times daily
- **Best times**: 8-9am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm (local time)
- **Hashtag limit**: 2-3 per tweet
- **Visual content**: Always include images or GIFs

### Instagram

- **Caption length**: 125-150 characters for feed, unlimited for Stories
- **Posting frequency**: 1-2 feed posts daily, 3-5 Stories daily
- **Best times**: 11am-1pm, 7-9pm
- **Hashtag limit**: 10-15 relevant hashtags
- **Visual style**: Bright, clean, aspirational

### TikTok

- **Video length**: 15-60 seconds (shorter performs better)
- **Posting frequency**: 1-2 videos daily
- **Best times**: 6-9pm, 7-9am
- **Caption**: Hook in first 3 seconds
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 trending + branded

## ✅ Best Practices

### Do's

✅ Respond to comments within 1 hour during business hours
✅ Use user-generated content (with permission)
✅ Tag users when featuring their trades
✅ Keep tone consistent across platforms
✅ Post at optimal times for your audience
✅ Use high-quality images and videos
✅ Include clear calls-to-action
✅ Track analytics and adjust strategy

### Don'ts

❌ Delete negative comments (respond professionally instead)
❌ Over-promote (follow 80/20 rule: 80% value, 20% promotion)
❌ Ignore mentions or tags
❌ Post without proofreading
❌ Use controversial or divisive topics
❌ Steal content without credit
❌ Post just for the sake of posting
❌ Ignore analytics and feedback
